Start Where You Are

AAC isn't specifically designed to teach you how to run ads. While we provide guides & resources to teach you how, AAC exists to provide structure & accountability to run them consistently. And you won't be figuring it out alone — you'll be surrounded by a community of action-focused solopreneurs doing the exact same work.

Members at $10/day and $100/day both belong here. The discipline is the same either way. * What is required: you must keep at least one active, spending ad campaign running every week — even at $1/day. Miss two consecutive weekly deadlines and your access is paused until you upload proof.

One Active Campaign, Every Window

Each submission window, you upload a screenshot of your Ads Manager showing at least one live campaign. No active ad = no credit for that window. No exceptions, no grace period, no "I meant to."

📸 Screenshot proof required each window

Your Numbers on the Form

Along with your screenshot, you fill out the submission form each window: spend, impressions, CTR, results. It takes two minutes and builds the habit of actually reading your dashboard instead of ignoring it.

📊 Spend · Impressions · CTR · Results

One Change Tied to Data

New ad creative. Updated copy. A budget shift. A new audience segment. Whatever the numbers are telling you — you act on it and log what you changed and why, directly on the submission form. This is how ad spend compounds.

✏️ Documented on the submission form

FAQ

Common Questions

Everything you need before you join. More questions? Reach out directly.

Why do I need AAC?
+
Because knowing you should run ads consistently and actually doing it are two completely different things. Most people start strong, hit a slow week, pause the campaign, and never restart with the same momentum. That cycle — start, stop, forget, restart — is exactly why ad spend feels like a drain instead of an investment. AAC exists to break that cycle. When you have a weekly proof submission on the line, peer accountability, and a leaderboard tracking your consistency, you show up. And when you show up every week — reviewing data, making changes, keeping campaigns live — your ad spend compounds instead of resetting to zero every few months. The question isn't whether you can afford to try AAC. It's whether you can afford to keep running ads the way you have been.
Do I need to already be running ads?
+
Not necessarily. We have guides & resources to help you get your first campaigns started — but we can't tell you specifically what to do for each campaign. We give you all the tools and resources to do that yourself.
Which platforms are supported?
+
Meta and Google Ads are the primary focus. TikTok, LinkedIn, and others are accepted as long as you can submit equivalent screenshot proof of an active campaign and weekly performance data.
What counts as a "documented change"?
+
Anything you changed based on what the data told you — new creative, updated copy, budget adjustment, new audience, bid strategy change. Describe what changed and why, and it counts.
Is there a minimum ad spend?
+
The minimum is $1/day — you need at least one active, spending campaign every week. Beyond that, the budget is yours. Members at $10/day and members at $1,000/day are both welcome. * What matters is consistency: miss two consecutive weekly proof deadlines and your access is paused until you upload proof. One week gets a warning. Two weeks gets a lockout.
Will Nate coach us on ad strategy?
+
Not in a 1-on-1 coaching sense — AAC isn't a coaching program. While we have guides & resources to help you set your own campaigns, we can't tell you specifically what to do. That's your job (or a hired professional). But we can encourage you to be consistent, give guidance & all the tools you need to succeed on your own so you don't give up if you don't see results immediately. Membership also includes monthly live strategy Zooms, guest speaker sessions with real practitioners, and peer ad reviews inside the community. You'll be surrounded by action-focused solopreneurs running ads alongside you — asking questions, sharing wins, and keeping each other accountable.
What happens if I miss a weekly deadline?
+
Miss one week and you get a warning. Miss a second consecutive week and your access is paused. You'll see a single-screen prompt to upload proof of an active campaign — once you do, access restores immediately and your 2-week window resets. You also lose All-Star eligibility for that calendar month.
Is this connected to SMAC and WAC?
+
Yes. AAC is the third challenge under The Accountability Challenge Co., alongside SMAC (social media) and WAC (websites). Same proof-based model, different discipline: paid ads.
